# System icon paths

The Linux, Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, CentOS, Arch Linux, openSUSE, Alpine Linux,
Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux, Kali Linux, FreeBSD, Red Hat, Linux Mint and Apple silhouettes in
`Components/SystemIconPaths.cs` are bundled from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons).

Attribution: icons supplied by Simple Icons contributors, based on the respective
projects' original marks. Paths are unchanged from the pinned SVGs; they are
rendered as monochrome icons at the UI's requested size. The Debian and Rocky
paths remain available under their respective share-alike licenses. Brand names and marks belong to their
respective owners; icons identify the connected system and imply no endorsement.
The four-pane Windows identifier is drawn locally from rectangle geometry.

All icon paths are compiled into the app and inherit the current foreground.
No images are downloaded at runtime.
